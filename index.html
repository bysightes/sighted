About Us

At Sightes, we specialize in fixation landscape designing, creating stunning gardens, compounds, homes, indoor and outdoor plants, decorations, and water networks. With more than 40 years of experience, all our projects are executed by professional engineers to ensure top-notch quality and precision. Our team is dedicated to bringing your vision to life with sustainable, beautiful, and functional landscapes that enhance your environment. We pride ourselves on using innovative techniques and high-quality materials to deliver projects that stand the test of time.

Our Services

Landscape Design & Fixation

We provide comprehensive landscape design and fixation services, tailoring outdoor spaces to your needs. Our engineers ensure structural integrity and aesthetic appeal, using advanced techniques for long-lasting results.

Garden & Compound Development

Transform barren areas into lush gardens and functional compounds. We handle everything from planning to execution, incorporating eco-friendly materials and designs that promote biodiversity.

Home & Indoor/Outdoor Plant Installation

Enhance your living spaces with expertly installed plants. Whether indoor or outdoor, we select and place vegetation that thrives in your climate, adding greenery and tranquility to your home.

Decoration & Water Networks

Our decoration services include custom features like fountains and lighting, while our water network expertise ensures efficient irrigation systems that conserve water and maintain healthy landscapes.
